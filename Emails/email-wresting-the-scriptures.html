When Quoting Scripture Falls Short of Being Scriptural

The Devil knows his Bible. That is not a comfortable thought, but it is a scriptural one. When Satan came to tempt Jesus in the wilderness, he did not come with open blasphemy or obvious error. He came with the Word of God on his lips — accurately quoted, properly referenced, and completely wrong.

The problem was not the text. The problem was what he was doing with it. This is the disturbing reality that runs through the whole of the New Testament: quoting scripture and speaking truth are not the same thing.
